# Example: HR Policy Assistant

## Summary

- Agent level: 1 (Knowledge Assistant)
- Purpose: Answer employee policy questions from approved HR documents
- Main risks: stale knowledge, overconfident answers, sensitive information disclosure

## Risk highlights

- Data risk: medium
- Instruction risk: low
- Business process risk: low

## Initial controls

- Restrict sources to approved HR policy set
- Add response disclaimer and escalation to HR contact
- Weekly source freshness check
- Basic usage logging

# Risk Taxonomy

This taxonomy provides a common risk language for agentic systems.

## 1. Autonomy risk

The agent acts without sufficient human oversight for the impact level.

## 2. Tool risk

The agent misuses internal or external tools, APIs, connectors, or workflows.

## 3. Permission risk

The agent has broader access than required for its intended purpose.

## 4. Instruction risk

The agent follows malicious, conflicting, or ambiguous instructions.

## 5. Memory risk

The agent stores, leaks, or reuses sensitive context inappropriately.

## 6. Data risk

The agent retrieves, exposes, or transforms sensitive data in unsafe ways.

## 7. Planning risk

The agent creates flawed multi-step plans that fail under real workflow constraints.

## 8. Delegation risk

The agent delegates tasks or invokes sub-agents without clear controls.

## 9. Observability risk

The organization cannot reliably reconstruct what happened.

## 10. Accountability risk

Ownership for agent decisions and incidents is unclear.

## 11. Compliance risk

The system violates legal, contractual, policy, or internal governance requirements.

## 12. Business process risk

The agent causes direct operational harm in critical workflows.

## How to use this taxonomy

1. Classify agent level first.
2. Identify top 3-5 risks from this list.
3. Map each risk to at least one concrete control.
4. Capture evidence requirements for each control.
